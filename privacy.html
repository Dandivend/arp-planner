ARP Planner: privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

ARP Planner is a personal day planner made for one household. It has no server, no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics.

What it stores, and where

  • On your device: your tasks, check-ins, cycle dates and settings, kept in your browser's storage for this app.
  • In your own Google Drive: one file, "ARP Planner data.json", so your phone and computer stay in sync. The app can only see files it created itself (the drive.file permission), not the rest of your Drive.
  • Sign-in tokens for Google and Microsoft stay on your device and are only sent to Google or Microsoft.

Google and Microsoft data

  • Google Calendar: the app reads the calendars you choose to show them in the planner, and adds planner items to its own "ARP Planner" calendar if you turn that on.
  • Google Drive: only the one sync file described above.
  • Microsoft To Do: the app reads and updates the lists you choose, so your to-dos appear in the planner and ticks go both ways.

This data is used only to show and sync your own planner. It is never sold, shared with anyone, used for ads, or used to train AI models. ARP Planner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The optional Claude helper

If you turn on AI integration in Settings and add your own Anthropic API key, the planner sends the tasks it needs (about two weeks back to three weeks ahead), this week's check-ins, and your work and sleep times to Anthropic's Claude API, only when the helper runs, so it can suggest and make changes. Your key stays on your device. Anthropic handles that data under its own privacy policy. It's off unless you turn it on.

Removing your data

Disconnect Google or Microsoft in the app's Settings, or remove access from your Google account or Microsoft account. Delete "ARP Planner data.json" from your Drive, and clear this site's data in your browser to remove what's on your device.
